Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · Rigvo is in pre-launch (early access).

Rigvo sets no cookies and uses no third-party trackers, analytics or advertising pixels. Fonts are served from our own server, not from Google.

We store a few small entries in your browser’s local storage. Essential entries are needed for the site to work; optional ones are stored only after you click “Accept” in the banner.

NameTypePurposeKept
rigvo_consent_v1EssentialRemembers your choice in the cookie bannerUntil you clear it or change your choice
rigvo_demoEssentialKeeps you signed in to the demo during your visitUntil you close the tab
rigvo_utmOptionalRemembers which campaign brought you here, sent with your requestUntil you close the tab
rigvo_demo_leadOptionalRemembers that you already filled the demo form, so we don’t ask againUntil you clear it

Change your choice

Open cookie settings at any time. Choosing “Essential only” deletes the optional entries.

Legal basis: Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive / § 25 TTDSG (Germany) — essential storage is strictly necessary; optional storage is based on your consent.
