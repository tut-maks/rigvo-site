Questions

How long does it take to get started?

You can send your first quote on day one. Send us your stock list and clients in Excel and we import them for you.

Where is my data stored?

In the European Union (Frankfurt), with daily backups. Prices and client details are visible only to the right people — enforced by the database, not just the screen.

Is there a mobile app?

Rigvo works in any phone browser today, including QR picking in the warehouse. Apps for iPhone and Android are on the way.

Yes. Monthly plans, no contract, and you can export all your data whenever you like.
